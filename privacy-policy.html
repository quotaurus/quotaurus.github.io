Website information

Quotaurus operates the public website at quotaurus.com. If you email Quotaurus, we will receive the information you choose to include in that email, such as your name, email address, organisation, and message.

Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and basic security logs. This is used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

How website information is used

  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the public website.
  • To respond to emails, support requests, privacy requests, and security reports.
  • To meet legal, security, and abuse-prevention obligations.

Sharing information

Quotaurus does not sell personal information. Website information may be shared with service providers that help operate hosting, email, support, or security infrastructure. Information may also be shared when required by law, to protect rights and security, or with your permission.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information. To make a request, email privacy@quotaurus.com.
