Privacy policy

How Quotaurus handles product information.

This policy covers the public Quotaurus website, RevFlow for Jira Cloud, and Muckr for iOS. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Who we are

Quotaurus operates the public website at quotaurus.com, RevFlow for Jira Cloud, and Muckr for iOS. You can contact Quotaurus about privacy questions at privacy@quotaurus.com.

Information the website collects

The public website is designed to be simple. It does not currently use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, account registration, or a contact form. If you email Quotaurus, we will receive the information you choose to include in that email, such as your name, email address, organisation, and message.

Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and basic security logs. This is used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Information RevFlow may process

When RevFlow is installed or used inside Jira, the app may process information needed to provide quote-to-delivery revenue control, including:

  • Atlassian site, installation, and account identifiers.
  • Jira project and issue keys, summaries, issue types, statuses, sprint metadata, parent or epic relationships, estimates, logged time, and field metadata needed by the app.
  • Jira user display names and account IDs for prepared-by, sender, subscriber, and permission workflows.
  • Quote records, versions, history, statuses, linked issue keys, clients, recipients, line items, values, notes, PO numbers, revenue streams, products, rates, quantities, tax settings, currencies, and forecasted completion dates entered by users.
  • Generated PDF quote content and sent-quote metadata, such as recipient email address and sent date.
  • Recurring quote templates, client/product/revenue-stream settings, exchange rates, report email settings, billing export profiles, and RevFlow project configuration.
  • Setup import/export data selected by administrators.
  • Support messages, diagnostic details, and screenshots you send to Quotaurus.

RevFlow does not intentionally store Jira issue descriptions except where a user creates a quote from Jira work and chooses to include issue-derived notes in quote content.

Information Muckr may process

When Muckr is installed or used, the app may process information needed to provide household task sharing, rewards, and Pay Day workflows, including:

  • Supabase authentication identifiers, email addresses, and Sign in with Apple identifiers where Apple sign-in is used.
  • Household names, invite codes, household settings, pay day settings, reward modes, currencies, rates, custom reward units, plan status, and household membership limits.
  • Member display names, roles, status, avatar URLs, optional payment links, and household membership records.
  • Task titles, descriptions, checklists, estimated minutes, due dates, recurrence settings, assignees, statuses, created-by records, and Stored Task templates.
  • Task ratings, stars, reward multipliers, settled task status, household stats, and Pay Day ledger information.
  • Photos or videos users choose to upload as task guides, plus profile photos users choose to upload as avatars.
  • Expo push tokens and notification metadata used to send task notifications, where notification permission is granted.
  • App Store subscription entitlement status for Muckr Premium. Payment card details are handled by Apple, not by Quotaurus.
  • Support messages, diagnostic details, and screenshots you send to Quotaurus.

Muckr asks for photo library access only when a user chooses to add a task guide or profile photo. Muckr asks for notification permission before registering a device for push notifications. Auth sessions are stored on the device using secure device storage.

Muckr media files are stored in Supabase Storage. The current app uses public media URLs for uploaded task media and avatars, so users should avoid uploading sensitive images or videos that are not needed for household task coordination.

How information is used

Quotaurus uses information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Quotaurus products.
  • Create, link, report on, export, and manage quote records inside Jira.
  • Generate quote PDFs and, when configured, send quote emails through Mailgun or SendGrid.
  • Create households, manage members, plan tasks, attach guides, calculate rewards, send notifications, and support Pay Day workflows in Muckr.
  • Manage Muckr Premium entitlement status and restore purchases through the App Store.
  • Respond to support, security, and privacy requests.
  • Troubleshoot bugs and investigate reliability issues.
  • Meet legal, security, and abuse-prevention obligations.

Sharing information

Quotaurus does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website, email, hosting, support, or app infrastructure. Information may also be shared when required by law, to protect rights and security, or with your permission.

RevFlow runs in the Atlassian ecosystem. Atlassian may process information according to Atlassian's own terms and privacy policies when you use Jira or install apps from the Atlassian Marketplace.

When optional quote email sending is configured and used, recipient details, email content, and quote PDF attachments are sent to the configured Mailgun or SendGrid service for delivery.

Muckr uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, file storage, and backend functions. Muckr may use Expo's push notification service to send task notifications to registered devices. Apple processes Sign in with Apple and App Store subscription purchases according to Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

RevFlow data remains in Forge app storage until users delete it, the app is uninstalled, or a retention or deletion process is requested and completed.

Muckr household data remains in Supabase until it is deleted in the app, an account is deleted, or a deletion request is completed. Muckr includes an account deletion action in settings. If a user is the only active Task Master for a household with other active members, Muckr may require the user to hand over or resolve that household first so other members' data is not deleted unexpectedly.

Quotaurus keeps support information only for as long as needed to provide support, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information. To make a request, email privacy@quotaurus.com.

Security

Quotaurus uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. RevFlow uses Atlassian Forge storage and Jira permission controls. Muckr uses Supabase access rules, household-scoped database policies, HTTPS, and secure device storage for auth sessions. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support emails.

Children

RevFlow is intended for business and professional use. Muckr is a household app and may be used by families. Household organisers are responsible for deciding who to invite, supervising younger users where appropriate, and avoiding unnecessary sensitive information in task names, notes, photos, or videos.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app, website, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of the policy shows when it was last updated.
