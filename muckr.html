The problem

Chores are easy to list. Fairness is harder.

Shared homes often run on reminders, memory, and half-finished lists. Muckr gives everyone the same view of what needs doing, who picked it up, what “done” means, and what has been earned by the time Pay Day arrives.

How it works

A household task loop that does not rely on nagging.

01

Create or join a household

Start a household as the organiser, or join an existing one with an invite code. Each member signs in on their own phone.

02

Plan the work

Add one-off chores, daily tasks, weekly jobs, fortnightly routines, monthly work, and Stored Tasks for jobs you use again and again.

03

Show what good looks like

Add descriptions, checklists, photos, or videos so everyone knows what needs doing before they claim the task.

04

Claim, complete, and rate

Helpers claim open tasks and mark them done. Task Masters can rate completed work without rating their own tasks.

05

Reward your way

Use real money, a custom reward unit, or “just tasks”. Values can be based on time and rate, or set as a flat amount per task.

06

Settle on Pay Day

Muckr totals rated tasks into a household ledger. Payment links can help the organiser pay people in their own banking app.

Inside the app

Everything a household needs to stay coordinated.

Task flow

Tasks move from open to settled.

  • Open tasks are up for grabs.
  • Claimed tasks show who is doing the work.
  • Completed work waits for a Task Master rating.
  • Rated tasks roll into Pay Day until they are settled.
Premium

Upgrade when the household grows.

  • Up to eight household members.
  • Multiple Task Masters.
  • Planning ahead and looking back across weeks.
  • Unlimited Stored Tasks, recurring tasks, reports, backgrounds, and accent colours.

Muckr Premium uses App Store subscriptions. Live prices and trial availability are shown by Apple during purchase.

Who it is for

Built for real households, not perfect routines.

Families

Give children and adults a shared task board, clear expectations, and fair reward tracking.

Housemates

Keep shared jobs visible without a group chat turning into a chore argument.

Task Masters

Plan work, assign or pause members, set reward rules, and settle Pay Day from one place.

Helpers

See available work, claim tasks, follow guides, and track what has been earned.

Mixed reward households

Use money, points, sweets, screen-time tokens, or no reward at all.

Visual learners

Attach photos and videos so “clean”, “done”, and “good enough” are easier to understand.
