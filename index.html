The problem

Your scope lives in Jira. Your revenue lives everywhere else.

Jira holds the work, but the commercial story is scattered across spreadsheets, PDFs, email threads, and finance exports. By the time numbers reconcile, the money's already been decided. RevFlow keeps quotes, issue links, statuses, forecast dates, PO numbers, line items, and reporting right next to the work that earns them — so nothing gets lost between delivery and the invoice.

How it works

Quote, configure, report, repeat — all inside Jira.

01

Start from the work itself

Build and manage quotes straight from the RevFlow project page or the Jira issue panel, and keep the linked quote in view right beside the delivery scope.

02

Set it up once, your way

A guided wizard maps Jira fields, statuses, issue types, currency, tax, quote items, clients, revenue streams, email, and access rules to how your team already works — no raw field IDs, no guesswork.

03

Track every quote to delivery

Map your quote statuses and work buckets — order, WIP, invoiceable, closed, lost — so every report mirrors the real Jira workflow instead of a finance team's best guess.

04

Catch revenue before it leaks

RevFlow flags current and next-sprint work with no quote attached, so you spot unbilled delivery while there's still time to do something about it.

05

Send polished quotes in a click

Generate client-ready quote PDFs on demand. Turn on optional Mailgun or SendGrid sending and RevFlow records the recipient and sent date right on the quote.

06

Put retainers on autopilot

Spin up monthly draft quotes from recurring templates for retainers, subscriptions, support packages, and any other work that repeats like clockwork.

FAQ

Answers before you install.

Who is behind RevFlow?

Quotaurus. RevFlow for Jira is our first product, with more revenue tools for Atlassian teams on the way.

Is there a free version?

Yes — RevFlow is free for up to 10 users, with a 30-day trial on paid tiers. Billing runs through the Atlassian Marketplace.

Is RevFlow Jira Cloud only?

Yes. RevFlow is a native Atlassian Forge app for Jira Cloud project and issue views.

Is there a setup wizard?

Yes. Project admins run a first-run wizard and can relaunch it any time from settings.

Does RevFlow support Jira dark mode?

Yes. It follows Jira theme support with dedicated dark-mode styling throughout.

Can one quote cover multiple Jira issues?

Yes. Open quotes link to as many Jira issues as the work needs, and show from each issue panel.

Can I create a PDF without sending email?

Absolutely. PDF generation is fully independent of optional Mailgun or SendGrid sending.

Does it handle VAT and multiple currencies?

Yes — configurable VAT settings plus GBP, USD, and EUR currency options.

Can I reuse my setup across projects?

Yes. RevFlow exports and imports setup as JSON for review and repeat rollout.
